Dart or glider: which paper airplane should you make?飞镖还是滑翔机：该折哪一款？

Pick a plane by what you want it to do and where you will throw it. Darts punch forward; gliders trade speed for time aloft.按飞行目标和场地选机型。飞镖冲得远；滑翔机用速度换滞空。

Paper Planes World · 3 min read纸飞机机库 · 约 3 分钟

In short:一句话： Want distance in a hallway? Start with a dart such as Basic Dart or Lift Off. Want slow indoor float? Pick a glider such as Stealth Glider. Want tricks? Choose an acrobatic design and trim the trailing edges up. 走廊测远距？从 Basic Dart 或 Lift Off 这类飞镖开始。室内慢漂？选 Stealth Glider 一类滑翔机。玩特技？选特技机并把后缘上折。

Darts: speed and distance飞镖：速度与远距

Darts have narrow noses and swept wings that cut drag. They like a firm, level throw and reward sharp centre creases. On this site, Basic Dart is the easiest entry; Lift Off and Zip Dart push farther once your folds are clean.

飞镖机头窄、机翼后掠，阻力小。适合有力水平投，中缝越干净越好。站内 Basic Dart 最易上手；折痕到位后再挑战 Lift Off、Zip Dart。

Gliders: hang time滑翔机：滞空

Gliders spread more wing area and fly slower. They need a gentle release and careful trailing-edge trim — too much upward bend stalls them. Stealth Glider and Sea Glider are strong hang-time picks for calm indoor air.

滑翔机翼面积更大、飞得更慢。需要轻投和谨慎的后缘微调——上折过多会失速。Stealth Glider、Sea Glider 适合无风室内练滞空。

Stunts and hybrids特技与混合

Acrobatic designs such as Loop Plane and Boomerang use trim and launch angle for loops or turns. Hybrids that list both distance and time aloft sit between dart and glider — useful when you want one plane for mixed games.

Loop Plane、Boomerang 等特技机靠微调与投角做筋斗或转弯。同时标了远距与滞空的混合机型介于两者之间，适合综合游戏。

Best beginner paper airplane最适合新手的纸飞机

If you want one first fold, use Basic Dart: Easy difficulty, no scissors, clear steps. Kids and classrooms should stay on Easy designs until symmetry becomes automatic, then move to Medium gliders.

若只折第一架，选 Basic Dart：简单难度、不用剪刀、步骤清楚。孩子与课堂应先练到对称成为习惯，再进入中等滑翔机。

Quick answers快速问答

What is the easiest paper airplane to make?最好折的纸飞机是哪款？

Basic Dart is the easiest starter in this collection: straight folds, Easy difficulty, and no scissors. It also flies far enough to feel rewarding on the first try.

Basic Dart 是站内最易上手的入门款：直线折法、简单难度、不用剪刀，飞得也够远，第一次就有成就感。

Dart or glider — which flies farther?飞镖和滑翔机哪个飞得更远？

Darts usually win pure forward distance with a firm level throw. Gliders travel less far but stay up longer. Pick the metric that matches your game.

在有力水平投下，飞镖通常更远。滑翔机距离较短但滞空更久。按你的比赛规则选指标。
