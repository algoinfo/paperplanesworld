How a paper airplane flies without an engine没有发动机，纸飞机怎么飞起来

Lift, drag, weight and the push from your hand explain almost every flight you see. Once you can name the forces, trim stops feeling like guessing.升力、阻力、重力与你出手的推力，几乎能解释你看到的所有飞行。能叫出力的名字，微调就不再靠猜。

Paper Planes World · 4 min read纸飞机机库 · 约 4 分钟

In short:一句话： Your throw is the only thrust. Wings make lift while air resists as drag, and weight pulls down. Balance these four and the plane glides; tip the balance and you get dives, stalls or turns. 出手是唯一推力。机翼产生升力，空气带来阻力，重力向下。四力平衡就滑翔；失衡就出现俯冲、失速或转弯。

The four forces四种力

Thrust comes only from the launch. Lift rises when air moves faster over a wing or meets it at a useful angle. Drag slows the plane; weight pulls toward the floor. After release, the plane is always trading height and speed — there is no continuous power.

推力只来自投掷。气流更快掠过机翼或以合适迎角撞上时产生升力。阻力减速，重力向下。出手后飞机一直在交换高度与速度——没有持续动力。

Centre of gravity重心

The balance point decides whether the nose stays pointed into the glide. Too far forward and the plane dives; too far aft and it stalls or loops. Nose folds, paper clips, and trailing-edge bends all shift this balance a little.

平衡点决定机头能否对准滑翔路径。太靠前会俯冲；太靠后会失速或翻跟头。机头折层、回形针与后缘微调都会轻微移动重心。

How the forces show up in flight这些力在飞行里长什么样

A dive is weight winning early. A stall is lift collapsing after too steep an angle. A steady turn is uneven lift between the wings. Use the fix-a-flight guide to match the pattern to one trim change.

俯冲是重力过早占优。失速是迎角过大后升力崩溃。稳定转弯是两侧升力不均。用修正飞行指南把模式对应到一处微调。

Quick answers快速问答

How do paper airplanes fly?纸飞机怎么飞？

A paper airplane flies by converting the energy of your throw into a glide. Wings produce lift while moving through air; drag and weight pull it down. Without an engine, every flight is a controlled descent.

纸飞机把投掷能量转成滑翔。机翼在空气中运动产生升力，阻力与重力把它往下拉。没有发动机，每次飞行都是受控下落。

What is the centre of gravity on a paper airplane?纸飞机的重心是什么？

It is the balance point of the folded sheet. Support the plane on one finger under the body to find it. Shifting that point forward or aft changes whether the plane dives or stalls.

就是折叠后整架飞机的平衡点。用一根手指托住机身下方即可找到。重心前后移动，会改变飞机更容易俯冲还是失速。
