Silent High Glider Paper Airplane

Try throwing it from a balcony or other high location to watch it soar for a long time. It is a intermediate fold for long floating glides, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 16–22 m (52–72 ft), ranking #7 of 37 for distance; roughly 10–13 s of hang time (#1 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Silent High Glider fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 16–22 metres (about 52–72 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #7. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Silent High Glider stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Silent High Glider should hold about 10–13 seconds of hang time. That places it #1 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Silent High Glider best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 10 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Silent High Glider?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
