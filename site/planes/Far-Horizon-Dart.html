Far-Horizon Dart Paper Airplane

This reliable design likes to stay up in the air and fly for a long long ways. It is a master fold for far, straight throws, shown in 11 steps (folds with hands only).

Metres, hang-time seconds, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table below — before the folding steps — with the fixed test protocol.

How to throw
Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Make a vertical centre crease, open it, then bring both top corners into the centre.

2Step 2

Bring the tip to the bottom edge — on A4, stop about 2 cm short of the edge.

3Step 3

Fold the tip back up level with the triangle flaps, with about 3 cm of overlap.

4Step 4

Fold the upper right into the centre, then pull the lower right out sideways and flatten; mirror on the left.

5Step 5

Reach in, pull the centre flaps out, refold them the opposite way, then fold the sides in.

6Step 6

Fold each centre flap's bottom edge back at a slight angle, tuck it under, and press flat.

7Step 7

Fold the outer points of the top triangle shape to the center as shown in the guide.

8Step 8

Close the airplane toward yourself.

9Step 9

Fold out both wings as in the photo. The body should taper back from a point to about 4cm at the tail.

10Step 10

Fold out one of the inner flaps at the front (where the cockpit is) and then fold it over the second flap to lock it in place as shown in the guide.

11Step 11

Finally, fold up the outer edges of the wings as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Far-Horizon Dart fly?

Our measured hangar result is 13–18 metres (about 43–59 feet), ranking #16 of 37 distance-rated designs. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Far-Horizon Dart stay in the air?

Our measured hang time is 8–10 seconds, #3 of 36 for time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Far-Horizon Dart best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is master. Use Letter & A4, 11 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Far-Horizon Dart?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
