The Bird Paper Airplane

Bird-like wing tips give The Bird a stable, fast track through the air. It is an advanced fold aimed at far, straight throws, shown in 6 clear steps (folds with hands only).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 20–26 m (66–85 ft), ranking #2 of 37 for distance; roughly 5–6 s of hang time (#16 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Swing the top-right corner diagonally across to the far side.

2Step 2

Flatten the sheet and repeat the fold on the other side.

3Step 3

Fold the top down so the new crease crosses the middle of the X.

4Step 4

Open the sheet and press it completely flat.

5Step 5

Next fold the top edge down until it meets the first horizontal crease.

6Step 6

Fold that top band down again on the horizontal crease.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the The Bird fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 20–26 metres (about 66–85 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #2. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the The Bird stay in the air?

A well-trimmed The Bird should hold about 5–6 seconds of hang time. That places it #16 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the The Bird best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is advanced. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the The Bird?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
