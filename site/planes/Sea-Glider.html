Sea Glider

You can experiment to find your own ways to bend the wings and how much weight to add to the front.

中等 Letter & A4 6 步 装饰需要剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the paper diagonally.

2步骤 2

Cut off the extra piece at the bottom.

3步骤 3

Fold the closed edge over about 1 inch (2.5 cm).

4步骤 4

Fold the plane in half.

5步骤 5

Fold one of the top edges over as shown.

6步骤 6

另一侧重复同样操作。

Repeat with the other side.
