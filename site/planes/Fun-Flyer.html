困难 Letter & A4 6 步 远距不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the top right corner to the left side.

2步骤 2

Repeat the last step with the other side.

3步骤 3

Open the sheet back up and accordion fold the sides into the center.

4步骤 4

Fold the bottom ends of the triangle shape to the top point.

5步骤 5

Fold down both flaps of the diamond shape, then unfold.

6步骤 6

Fold the top edges of the diamond shape to the middle and then unfold.
