Fun Flyer Paper Airplane

Try throwing it straight up and see if you can catch it on the way down. It is a advanced fold for far, straight throws, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 11–15 m (36–49 ft), ranking #22 of 37 for distance; roughly 4–5 s of hang time (#30 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Fun Flyer fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 11–15 metres (about 36–49 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #22. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Fun Flyer stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Fun Flyer should hold about 4–5 seconds of hang time. That places it #30 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Fun Flyer best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is advanced. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Fun Flyer?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
