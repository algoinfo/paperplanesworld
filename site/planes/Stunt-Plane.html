Stunt Plane

Adjust the wingtips to make it spin or flip. If you throw it the right way, it will spin into the air.

困难 Letter & A4 6 步 特技不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the corners down to each side and unfold, making an X pattern.

2步骤 2

Fold the top down so the crease lines up with the center of the X pattern.

3步骤 3

Fold the top two corners to the center line.

4步骤 4

Unfold the last step and then tuck the sides inside with an accordion fold.

5步骤 5

Fold in the sides of the bottom layer. Note that the crease goes right through the bottom corners.

6步骤 6

Now, fold the big triangle in half, away from you and then fold it back up again so a little bit is poking up.
