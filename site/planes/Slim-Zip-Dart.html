Slim Zip Dart Paper Airplane

The thin body and front stabilizing wings allows it to quickly zip through the air and travel long distances. It is a advanced fold for long floating glides, shown in 8 steps (folds with hands only).

Metres, hang-time seconds, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table below — before the folding steps — with the fixed test protocol.

Flight notes

Best for
Long floats — long floating glides
Folding skill
Advanced
Paper & folds
Letter & A4 · 8 folds · Hands only
How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

Metres, hang time, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table before the folding steps.

Measured results

Hangar figures for this design — distance in metres, hang time in seconds, and rank among scored planes — before you fold.

Metric Measured result Hangar rank
Distance 13–17 m (43–56 ft) #18 of 37
Hang time 4–5 s #28 of 36

Test conditions (same for every design we score): 70–80 gsm Letter or A4 printer paper; standing release at about 1.6 m shoulder height; calm indoor air (no wind); each thrower gets 10 attempts and we keep their best throw. Ranks compare only designs that cleared the scoring threshold under this protocol.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Fold the corner across to the other side of the sheet.

2Step 2

Open it, repeat with the other corner, then open again.

3Step 3

Accordion-tuck the sides inside on the creases you already made.

4Step 4

Fold the top layer of the side corners up to the top edge.

5Step 5

Fold both outer edges into the centre, tucking them under the diamond.

6Step 6

Close the paper plane along the spine.

7Step 7

Fold one wing down from the nose of the plane to the center of the tail. Mirror the fold on the opposite side.

8Step 8

Fold down the front mini-wings to complete this design as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Slim Zip Dart fly?

Our measured hangar result is 13–17 metres (about 43–56 feet), ranking #18 of 37 distance-rated designs. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Slim Zip Dart stay in the air?

Our measured hang time is 4–5 seconds, #28 of 36 for time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Slim Zip Dart best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is advanced. Use Letter & A4, 8 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Slim Zip Dart?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
