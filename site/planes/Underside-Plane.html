Underside Plane

The extra underside flaps give the wings rigidity and help it glide better.

中等 Letter & A4 6 步 滞空不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the paper in half vertically and horizontally, then open it back up.

2步骤 2

Holding the paper horizontally, fold the top edge down to meet the center crease.

3步骤 3

Now, fold the top edge down at the center crease.

4步骤 4

Fold the top right corner over, down to the center fold as shown.

5步骤 5

Repeat with the left side.

6步骤 6

Again, fold the corner down to meet the horizontal fold.
