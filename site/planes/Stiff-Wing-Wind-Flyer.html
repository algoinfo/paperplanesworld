Stiff-Wing Wind Flyer Paper Airplane

Its wide stiff wings allow it to fly in a strong wind. It is a intermediate fold for long floating glides, shown in 6 steps (needs a small cut).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 10–14 m (33–46 ft), ranking #24 of 37 for distance; roughly 4–6 s of hang time (#21 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease both corners down and open them to form an X.

2Step 2

Fold the top so the crease crosses the middle of the X.

3Step 3

Bring both top corners in so they meet the midline.

4Step 4

Open those folds and accordion-tuck the sides into the middle.

5Step 5

Lift the left and right points up to the top as shown.

6Step 6

Fold the top down to the base of the triangle.

7Step 7

Bring the halves together toward yourself.

8Step 8

Fold out both sides to create the wings. The body should be about a 1/2 inch tall (1.3cm) as shown in the guide.

9Step 9

Finally, cut two small flaps at the tail of each wing as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Stiff-Wing Wind Flyer fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 10–14 metres (about 33–46 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #24. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Stiff-Wing Wind Flyer stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Stiff-Wing Wind Flyer should hold about 4–6 seconds of hang time. That places it #21 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Stiff-Wing Wind Flyer best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 9 steps, needs a cut.

How should I throw the Stiff-Wing Wind Flyer?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
