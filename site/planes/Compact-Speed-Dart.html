Compact Speed Dart Paper Airplane

This compact paper airplane makes up for its size with speed. It is a advanced fold for long floating glides, shown in 17 steps (folds with hands only).

The Compact Speed Dart is built more for shape and handling than for raw range or hang time. See the measured results table for how we score designs that do fly for distance or hang time.

Flight notes

Best for
Long floats — long floating glides
Folding skill
Advanced
Paper & folds
Letter & A4 · 17 folds · Hands only
How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

Metres, hang time, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table before the folding steps.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease the sheet vertically down the middle.

2Step 2

Open the sheet and crease it horizontally in half.

3Step 3

Hold the folded packet with the opening at the bottom, then fold both edges into the centre.

4Step 4

Open the last two folds, then fold the top corner down to the first crease.

5Step 5

Mirror the same fold on the opposite side.

6Step 6

Now fold the top-right edge down onto the midline.

7Step 7

Do the same fold on the left.

8Step 8

Open those last steps back up and accordion fold the right side in as shown in the guide.

9Step 9

Mirror the same fold on the opposite side.

10Step 10

Take the top layer of both outer edges and line them up with the diagonal creases as shown in the guide.

11Step 11

Fold them in again along the diagonal creases as shown in the guide.

12Step 12

Flip the plane around and repeat steps 10 and 11 on this side as shown in the guide.

13Step 13

Fold the tip about an inch (2.5cm) down as in the photo.

14Step 14

Now close the plane toward yourself.

15Step 15

Fold down one wing. The body will be about 0.5 inch (1.25cm) tall as shown in the guide.

16Step 16

Mirror the same fold on the opposite side.

17Step 17

Finally, fold the tail fins up as in the photo.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Compact Speed Dart fly?

The Compact Speed Dart is not scored for distance in this hangar — it is not built for range. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Compact Speed Dart stay in the air?

The Compact Speed Dart is not scored for hang time here — the design is not aimed at time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Compact Speed Dart best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is advanced. Use Letter & A4, 17 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Compact Speed Dart?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
