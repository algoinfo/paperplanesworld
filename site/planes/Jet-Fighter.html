Jet Fighter Paper Airplane

When adjusted correctly, this paper jet can glide for long distances. It is a advanced fold for long floating glides, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 9–12 m (30–39 ft), ranking #31 of 37 for distance; roughly 4–6 s of hang time (#19 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease vertically in half, open it, then fold both corners down to the middle.

2Step 2

Take one top edge and fold it down to the bottom edge on that same side.

3Step 3

Open it and repeat step two on the other side to match the creases in the photo.

4Step 4

Fold the top down while accordion-tucking the sides inward together.

5Step 5

Fold the bottom triangle back upward.

6Step 6

Fold both outer triangle points back, tucking the folds between the layers.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Jet Fighter fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 9–12 metres (about 30–39 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #31. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Jet Fighter stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Jet Fighter should hold about 4–6 seconds of hang time. That places it #19 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Jet Fighter best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is advanced. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Jet Fighter?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
