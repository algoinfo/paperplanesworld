Jet Fighter

When adjusted correctly, this paper jet can glide for long distances.

困难 Letter & A4 6 步 远距滞空不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the paper in half vertically. Then, open and fold both corners down to the middle.

2步骤 2

Take one top edge and fold down to the bottom edge on the same side.

3步骤 3

Unfold and repeat step two on the other side to get the creases as shown in the picture.

4步骤 4

Fold the top down and accordion fold the sides in at the same time.

5步骤 5

Fold the bottom triangular piece back up.

6步骤 6

Fold back both outer points of the triangle shape. Be sure the folds are tucked in-between both layers.
