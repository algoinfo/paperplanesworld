How to throw
Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward. 让机身保持水平，用平稳有力的推送直线投出，不要向上抛。

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Fold the sheet in half, unfold and then fold the top two corners to the center.

2Step 2

3Step 3

4Step 4

5Step 5

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Wilson fly?Wilson 能飞多远？

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 16–22 metres (about 52–72 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #6. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

在无风天气、水平投掷的情况下，大约能飞 大约 16–22 米。在站内 37 款远距机型中排第 6。风力、纸张克重和折痕误差都会改变实际结果。

How long does the Wilson stay in the air?Wilson 能滞空多久？

A well-trimmed Wilson should hold about 3–4 seconds of hang time. That places it #34 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

调整得当的 Wilson 可以保持大约 3–4 秒。在站内 36 款机型中滞空排第 34。从更高的位置轻投、保持两翼水平，都能延长滞空时间。

What type of paper airplane is the Wilson?Wilson 属于什么类型的纸飞机？

It is a distance and acrobatic stunt plane rated Easy difficulty. Fold it from Letter & A4 in 6 steps with no scissors required.

它是一款远距特技机型，难度为简单，用Letter & A4折 6 步，全程不用剪刀。

How should I throw the Wilson?Wilson 应该怎么投掷？

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.

让机身保持水平，用平稳有力的推送直线投出，不要向上抛。
