Wilson Paper Airplane

This design has a tight and narrow nose with wings that flair out in the back. It is a beginner fold for far, straight throws, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 16–22 m (52–72 ft), ranking #6 of 37 for distance; roughly 3–4 s of hang time (#34 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

Folding steps

2Step 2

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Wilson fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 16–22 metres (about 52–72 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #6. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Wilson stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Wilson should hold about 3–4 seconds of hang time. That places it #34 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Wilson best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is beginner. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Wilson?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
