The Buzz Paper Airplane

Bend the tail flaps up or down to control the flight. It is a intermediate fold for long floating glides, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 9–12 m (30–39 ft), ranking #30 of 37 for distance; roughly 5–6 s of hang time (#17 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease the sheet down the middle.

2Step 2

Open the sheet, then bring the top corners to the midline.

3Step 3

Fold the tip down to the edge of the previous fold.

4Step 4

Fold the upper flanks in onto the midline.

5Step 5

Fold the top back about 1/2 inch (13 mm) away from you.

6Step 6

Bring the halves together toward yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the The Buzz fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 9–12 metres (about 30–39 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #30. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the The Buzz stay in the air?

A well-trimmed The Buzz should hold about 5–6 seconds of hang time. That places it #17 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the The Buzz best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the The Buzz?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
