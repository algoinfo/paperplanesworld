Spinner Plane

Designed to spin through the air with amazing aerobatics.

中等 Letter & A4 6 步 特技装饰不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the top right corner to the left side.

2步骤 2

Open the sheet back up and repeat the same step with the left corner.

3步骤 3

Open the sheet back up and accordion fold both sides in so it looks like this.

4步骤 4

Next, fold the bottom layer to the center.

5步骤 5

Repeat with the left side.

6步骤 6

Now, fold the right side of the triangle shape to the top point.
