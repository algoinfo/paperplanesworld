Quick Floater Paper Airplane

A quick flyer. It is a advanced fold for long floating glides, shown in 9 steps (folds with hands only).

Metres, hang-time seconds, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table below — before the folding steps — with the fixed test protocol.

Flight notes

Best for
Long floats — long floating glides
Folding skill
Advanced
Paper & folds
Letter & A4 · 9 folds · Hands only
How to throw
Launch softly at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high point, and let the plane glide instead of forcing it.

Metres, hang time, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table before the folding steps.

Measured results

Hangar figures for this design — distance in metres, hang time in seconds, and rank among scored planes — before you fold.

Metric Measured result Hangar rank
Distance 12–16 m (39–52 ft) #20 of 37
Hang time 3–4 s #32 of 36

Test conditions (same for every design we score): 70–80 gsm Letter or A4 printer paper; standing release at about 1.6 m shoulder height; calm indoor air (no wind); each thrower gets 10 attempts and we keep their best throw. Ranks compare only designs that cleared the scoring threshold under this protocol.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease the sheet down the middle.

2Step 2

Bring the right corner to the bottom-centre of the left rectangle — on A4, stop 2 cm short of the bottom edge.

3Step 3

Open the sheet and repeat on the other corner to match the creases shown.

4Step 4

Open the sheet and accordion-fold the centre inward.

5Step 5

Now fold the outer flaps onto the midline.

6Step 6

Fold the centre tab upward as in the photo.

7Step 7

Take the outer points and fold them in twice so they're tucked in as shown in the guide.

8Step 8

Bring the halves together toward yourself.

9Step 9

Finally, fold out the edges to complete the plane. If you want, you can give it a fancy crinkle fold as pictured as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Quick Floater fly?

Our measured hangar result is 12–16 metres (about 39–52 feet), ranking #20 of 37 distance-rated designs. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Quick Floater stay in the air?

Our measured hang time is 3–4 seconds, #32 of 36 for time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Quick Floater best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is advanced. Use Letter & A4, 9 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Quick Floater?

Launch softly at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high point, and let the plane glide instead of forcing it.
