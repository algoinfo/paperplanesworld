Flapping-Wing Sprinter Paper Airplane

The wings will flap at certain angles, so experiment with it and throw gently. It is a intermediate fold for far, straight throws, shown in 7 steps (folds with hands only).

The Flapping-Wing Sprinter is built more for shape and handling than for raw range or hang time. See the measured results table for how we score designs that do fly for distance or hang time.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Bring opposite corners together to crease a diagonal.

2Step 2

Crease the long edge over by about 3/4 inch (19 mm).

3Step 3

Close the sheet toward yourself, then rotate so the thickest tip points up and the squared tip faces left.

4Step 4

Bring the right side over to match the left and press a vertical crease.

5Step 5

Fold the remaining side down until the edges meet.

6Step 6

Fold one outer flap down for the first wing; the body should sit about 3/4 inch (19 mm) tall.

7Step 7

Fold the other flap down to complete the plane as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Flapping-Wing Sprinter fly?

The Flapping-Wing Sprinter is not scored for distance in this hangar — it is not built for range. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Flapping-Wing Sprinter stay in the air?

The Flapping-Wing Sprinter is not scored for hang time here — the design is not aimed at time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Flapping-Wing Sprinter best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 7 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Flapping-Wing Sprinter?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
