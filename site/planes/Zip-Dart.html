Zip Dart

The thin body and front stabilizing wings allows it to quickly zip through the air and travel long distances.

困难 Letter & A4 6 步 远距滞空不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the corner down to the other side of the paper.

2步骤 2

Unfold and repeat with the other corner. Then unfold again.

3步骤 3

Accordion fold the sides inside using the creases made in the previous steps.

4步骤 4

Fold the top layer of the side corners up to the top.

5步骤 5

Take both outer edges and fold them to the center. Be sure they are tucked underneath the diamond shape.

6步骤 6

Fold the paper plane in half.
