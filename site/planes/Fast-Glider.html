Fast Glider Paper Airplane

The separate wing and tail sections make this one of the more realistic designs. It is a intermediate fold for long floating glides, shown in 6 steps (needs a small cut).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 12–16 m (39–52 ft), ranking #21 of 37 for distance; roughly 4–5 s of hang time (#22 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease in half, open it, then bring both top corners into the centre.

2Step 2

Fold the tip down to the edge of the previous folds.

3Step 3

Fold the top edges inward to the centre.

4Step 4

Bring the tip down until it meets the bottom edge.

5Step 5

Next fold the tip back up as shown — the doubled band should be about 1 inch (2.5 cm).

6Step 6

Close the plane in half and pick up scissors.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Fast Glider fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 12–16 metres (about 39–52 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #21. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Fast Glider stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Fast Glider should hold about 4–5 seconds of hang time. That places it #22 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Fast Glider best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, needs a cut.

How should I throw the Fast Glider?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
