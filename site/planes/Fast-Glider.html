How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard. 以略向上扬的角度轻轻放出，最好从高处投掷，让它自然进入滑翔，不要用力硬抛。

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Fold the sheet in half, unfold and then fold the top two corners to the center.

2Step 2

Fold the top point down to meet the edge of the previous folds.

3Step 3

Fold the top two edges in to the center.

4Step 4

Fold the top point down to the bottom edge.

5Step 5

Next, fold the point back up as shown. The doubled over part should be about 1 inch (2.5cm) tall.

6Step 6

Fold the plane in half and grab a pair of scissors.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Fast Glider fly?Fast Glider 能飞多远？

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 12–16 metres (about 39–52 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #21. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

在无风天气、水平投掷的情况下，大约能飞 大约 12–16 米。在站内 37 款远距机型中排第 21。风力、纸张克重和折痕误差都会改变实际结果。

How long does the Fast Glider stay in the air?Fast Glider 能滞空多久？

A well-trimmed Fast Glider should hold about 4–5 seconds of hang time. That places it #22 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

调整得当的 Fast Glider 可以保持大约 4–5 秒。在站内 36 款机型中滞空排第 22。从更高的位置轻投、保持两翼水平，都能延长滞空时间。

What type of paper airplane is the Fast Glider?Fast Glider 属于什么类型的纸飞机？

It is a distance and time aloft glider rated Medium difficulty. Fold it from Letter & A4 in 6 steps and keep a pair of scissors handy.

它是一款远距滞空机型，难度为中等，用Letter & A4折 6 步，需要准备剪刀。

How should I throw the Fast Glider?Fast Glider 应该怎么投掷？

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

以略向上扬的角度轻轻放出，最好从高处投掷，让它自然进入滑翔，不要用力硬抛。
