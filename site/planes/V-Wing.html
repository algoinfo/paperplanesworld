V-Wing Paper Airplane

Like a stealthy spy plane, this paper glider flies quietly through the air. It is a intermediate fold for long floating glides, shown in 6 steps (needs a small cut).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 15–20 m (49–66 ft), ranking #9 of 37 for distance; roughly 4–5 s of hang time (#25 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

Folding steps

Frequently asked questions

How far can the V-Wing fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 15–20 metres (about 49–66 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #9. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the V-Wing stay in the air?

A well-trimmed V-Wing should hold about 4–5 seconds of hang time. That places it #25 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the V-Wing best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, needs a cut.

How should I throw the V-Wing?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
