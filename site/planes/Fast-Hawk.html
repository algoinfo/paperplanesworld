Fast Hawk Paper Airplane

Like a hawk, this paper plane can glide over long distances. It is a master fold for long floating glides, shown in 6 steps (needs a small cut).

In calm air with a level throw, expect about 9–12 m (30–39 ft), ranking #28 of 37 for distance; roughly 5–7 s of hang time (#12 of 36). Treat the numbers as a trim target: if your fold lands short, check symmetry and crease sharpness first.

How to throw

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Fast Hawk fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 9–12 metres (about 30–39 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #28. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Fast Hawk stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Fast Hawk should hold about 5–7 seconds of hang time. That places it #12 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Fast Hawk best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is master. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, needs a cut.

How should I throw the Fast Hawk?

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.
