Midrange Straight Dart Paper Airplane

The Midrange Straight Dart is a intermediate paper airplane tuned for far, straight throws. Follow the 11-step diagram (folds with hands only).

Metres, hang-time seconds, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table below — before the folding steps — with the fixed test protocol.

Best for

Paper & folds
Letter & A4 · 11 folds · Hands only
How to throw
Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Hold the sheet upright, fold top to bottom, then fold left to right again.

2Step 2

Open the horizontal fold, then bring both top corners into the centre.

3Step 3

Fully open the sheet, then accordion-tuck the sides in.

4Step 4

Fold the top layer of the right flap fully to the left, then fold the remaining right flap into the centre.

5Step 5

You now have three layers on the left — fold the top layer into the centre.

6Step 6

Fold those two flaps upward in opposite directions as shown.

7Step 7

Repeat steps 4 and 5 with the left side as shown in the guide.

8Step 8

Now fold the flaps up in opposite directions, like in step 6 as shown in the guide.

9Step 9

Close the airplane along the centre spine.

10Step 10

Fold the wings out as in the photo. The edge of the front part of the wing should touch the bottom of the body of the airplane.

11Step 11

Finally, fold the top layer of wings up as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Midrange Straight Dart fly?

Our measured hangar result is 14–19 metres (about 46–62 feet), ranking #13 of 37 distance-rated designs. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Midrange Straight Dart stay in the air?

The Midrange Straight Dart is not scored for hang time here — the design is not aimed at time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Midrange Straight Dart best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 11 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Midrange Straight Dart?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
