Watch in awe as it executes a mesmerizing aerial dance, a thrilling adventure through the air, a spectacle of spin that promises to captivate and exhilarate.

困难 Letter & A4 6 步 特技不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the corners down to each side and unfold to make an X shape.

2步骤 2

Fold the top down and make a crease so it lines up with the middle of the X shape.

3步骤 3

Fold the top two corners to the center line.

4步骤 4

Unfold the last step and then accordion fold the sides so they are tucked inside.

5步骤 5

Fold the bottom corners of the triangle up to the top.

6步骤 6

Now, fold the top sides of the diamond to the center line.
