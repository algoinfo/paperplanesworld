Spin Plane Paper Airplane

Watch in awe as it executes a mesmerizing aerial dance, a thrilling adventure through the air, a spectacle of spin that promises to captivate and exhilarate. It is a advanced fold for tricks and loops, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

The Spin Plane is built more for shape and handling than for raw range or hang time.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease both corners down to the sides, then open them to leave an X.

2Step 2

Fold the top so the crease crosses the middle of the X.

3Step 3

Bring both top corners in so they meet the midline.

4Step 4

Undo the last fold, then accordion-tuck the sides inside.

5Step 5

Fold the triangle's bottom corners up to the top.

6Step 6

Now fold the diamond's top sides onto the midline.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Spin Plane fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect only a short distance, since this design is not built for range. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Spin Plane stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Spin Plane should hold a very brief flight; the design is not aimed at hang time. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Spin Plane best for?

It is best for tricks and loops. Folding skill is advanced. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Spin Plane?

Use a quick wrist snap and a fairly firm throw so the plane has enough speed to complete its maneuver.
