Boomerang

Boomerangs come in a variety of shapes and styles. This origami boomerang has the traditional shape and can be made from a single piece of paper with only one simple cut. It has some tricky steps, so we recommend downloading our printable template to make it easier.

专家 Letter & A4 6 步 特技需要剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the paper in half and then unfold. Cut along the fold and pick one side. You can save the other side to make a second boomerang.

2步骤 2

Fold the paper in half. Then unfold and fold both sides into the middle.

3步骤 3

Fold the paper in half the other direction and orient the folded edge at the bottom.

4步骤 4

Fold both corners into the middle and use your fingernail to make a crisp crease.

5步骤 5

Unfold the previous 2 steps, then open up the left flap and pinch the middle of the paper so it folds the other direction.

6步骤 6

Continue rotating and folding that pinched piece flat until you get an L shape with vertical walls.
