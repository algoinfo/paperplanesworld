How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard. 以略向上扬的角度轻轻放出，最好从高处投掷，让它自然进入滑翔，不要用力硬抛。

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Fold the paper in half and then unfold.

2Step 2

Fold the right side over to the left, unfold and repeat with the left side.

3Step 3

Unfold and then fold the top down so the fold goes through the X formed by all the other creases.

4Step 4

Unfold again and then fold the right side down to the diagonal crease.

5Step 5

Repeat with the left side.

6Step 6

Fold the top down to the bottom.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Mosquito fly?Mosquito 能飞多远？

With a calm day and a level throw, expect roughly 16–21 metres (about 52–69 feet). Among the 37 distance-rated designs in this collection it ranks #8. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

在无风天气、水平投掷的情况下，大约能飞 大约 16–21 米。在站内 37 款远距机型中排第 8。风力、纸张克重和折痕误差都会改变实际结果。

How long does the Mosquito stay in the air?Mosquito 能滞空多久？

A well-trimmed Mosquito should hold about 4–5 seconds of hang time. That places it #24 of 36 designs for time aloft. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

调整得当的 Mosquito 可以保持大约 4–5 秒。在站内 36 款机型中滞空排第 24。从更高的位置轻投、保持两翼水平，都能延长滞空时间。

What type of paper airplane is the Mosquito?Mosquito 属于什么类型的纸飞机？

It is a distance and time aloft glider rated Medium difficulty. Fold it from Letter & A4 in 6 steps with no scissors required.

它是一款远距滞空机型，难度为中等，用Letter & A4折 6 步，全程不用剪刀。

How should I throw the Mosquito?Mosquito 应该怎么投掷？

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

以略向上扬的角度轻轻放出，最好从高处投掷，让它自然进入滑翔，不要用力硬抛。
