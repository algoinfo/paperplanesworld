Aerial Spinner Paper Airplane

Designed to spin through the air with amazing aerobatics. It is a intermediate fold for tricks and loops, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

The Aerial Spinner is built more for shape and handling than for raw range or hang time.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Aerial Spinner fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect only a short distance, since this design is not built for range. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the Aerial Spinner stay in the air?

A well-trimmed Aerial Spinner should hold a very brief flight; the design is not aimed at hang time. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the Aerial Spinner best for?

It is best for tricks and loops. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 13 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Aerial Spinner?

Use a quick wrist snap and a fairly firm throw so the plane has enough speed to complete its maneuver.
