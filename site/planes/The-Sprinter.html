The Sprinter

The wings will flap at certain angles, so experiment with it and throw gently. This design is sensitive to asymmetries and takes some small adjustments to work well.

中等 Letter & A4 6 步 远距特技不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the paper in half diagonally so opposite corners meet.

2步骤 2

Fold the long edge about 3/4 inch (19 mm).

3步骤 3

Fold the paper in half towards you and then rotate so that the thickest point is facing up. The squared off point will be facing to the left.

4步骤 4

Fold the right side over and line it up with the left side and make a vertical crease.

5步骤 5

Fold the other side down so opposite sides meet.

6步骤 6

Now, fold one outer flap down to create the first wing. The body will be about 3/4 inch (19 mm) tall.
