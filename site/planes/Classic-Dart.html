Classic Dart Paper Airplane

This is one of the most iconic paper airplane designs, and also one of the easiest to fold. It is a beginner fold for far, straight throws, shown in 5 steps (folds with hands only).

Metres, hang-time seconds, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table below — before the folding steps — with the fixed test protocol.

Flight notes

Best for
Far throws — far, straight throws
Folding skill
Beginner
Paper & folds
Letter & A4 · 5 folds · Hands only
How to throw
Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.

Metres, hang time, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table before the folding steps.

Measured results

Hangar figures for this design — distance in metres, hang time in seconds, and rank among scored planes — before you fold.

Metric Measured result Hangar rank
Distance 17–23 m (56–75 ft) #5 of 37
Hang time 3–4 s #33 of 36

Test conditions (same for every design we score): 70–80 gsm Letter or A4 printer paper; standing release at about 1.6 m shoulder height; calm indoor air (no wind); each thrower gets 10 attempts and we keep their best throw. Ranks compare only designs that cleared the scoring threshold under this protocol.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease the sheet down the middle.

2Step 2

Open it flat, then tuck both top corners into the midline.

3Step 3

Bring the upper long edges in until they meet the centre crease.

4Step 4

Close the model in half along its length.

5Step 5

Fold each wing down so its edge lines up with the belly of the fuselage.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Classic Dart fly?

Our measured hangar result is 17–23 metres (about 56–75 feet), ranking #5 of 37 distance-rated designs. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Classic Dart stay in the air?

Our measured hang time is 3–4 seconds, #33 of 36 for time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Classic Dart best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is beginner. Use Letter & A4, 5 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Classic Dart?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
