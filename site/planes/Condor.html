Condor

A locking fold keeps this paper airplane together during flight. No tape, staples or paperclips required!

困难 Letter & A4 6 步 滞空不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold one corner down to meet the other side. Unfold and repeat with the other corner.

2步骤 2

Unfold and flip the paper over. Fold the top down so the new crease goes right through the cross made by the previous step.

3步骤 3

Unfold and flip the paper over. Accordion fold the sides into the middle and press flat.

4步骤 4

Fold the top point down to meet the edge of the top layer of paper.

5步骤 5

Unfold everything to the beginning. Then fold on the existing crease which is just under the middle of the paper.

6步骤 6

Fold that crease all the way over so it goes past the bottom.
