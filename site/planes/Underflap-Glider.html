Underflap Glider Paper Airplane

The extra underside flaps give the wings rigidity and help it glide better. It is a intermediate fold for long floating glides, shown in 11 steps (folds with hands only).

Metres, hang-time seconds, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table below — before the folding steps — with the fixed test protocol.

Flight notes

Best for
Long floats — long floating glides
Folding skill
Intermediate
Paper & folds
Letter & A4 · 11 folds · Hands only
How to throw
Launch softly at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high point, and let the plane glide instead of forcing it.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease the sheet both ways, then open it flat.

2Step 2

With the sheet landscape, fold the top edge down to the centre crease.

3Step 3

Now fold the top edge down on the centre crease.

4Step 4

Fold the top-right corner over down to the centre fold as shown.

5Step 5

Do the same fold on the left.

6Step 6

Fold that corner down again to meet the horizontal crease.

7Step 7

Repeat step six on the other side as shown in the guide.

8Step 8

Next, fold the top point down to meet the horizontal fold as shown in the guide.

9Step 9

Crease the sheet plane in half away from you.

10Step 10

Fold one wing out, leaving about 1 inch (2.5cm) for the body of the airplane as shown in the guide.

11Step 11

Finally, fold down the second wing to match as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Underflap Glider fly?

Our measured hangar result is 10–14 metres (about 33–46 feet), ranking #23 of 37 distance-rated designs. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Underflap Glider stay in the air?

The Underflap Glider is not scored for hang time here — the design is not aimed at time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Underflap Glider best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 11 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Underflap Glider?

Launch softly at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high point, and let the plane glide instead of forcing it.
