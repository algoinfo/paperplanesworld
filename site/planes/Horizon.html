Horizon

This reliable design likes to stay up in the air and fly for a long long ways.

专家 Letter & A4 6 步 远距不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold the paper in half vertically. Unfold and then fold the top two corners to the center.

2步骤 2

Fold the top point down to the bottom edge. If you are using A4 paper, fold it about 2cm shy of the bottom edge.

3步骤 3

Fold the point back up, even with the bottom of the triangle flaps, about 3cm of overlap.

4步骤 4

Fold the top part of the right side to the center and then take the bottom part of the right side and stretch it to the side and flatten it out. Repeat with the left side.

5步骤 5

Reach inside and pull out the center flaps. Then refold them in the opposite direction and fold the sides in.

6步骤 6

Take the bottom edge of each center flap and fold it backwards at a slight angle and then tuck under and press flat.
