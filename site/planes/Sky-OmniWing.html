Sky OmniWing

This design is an improved version of the original Proto OmniWing, designed by Mike Kelsey. It features a unique construction that uses tape and layered pieces to produce a well engineered structural aircraft that has low drag and can float for a long time.

How to throw
Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard. 以略向上扬的角度轻轻放出，最好从高处投掷，让它自然进入滑翔，不要用力硬抛。

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Hold the paper so the long edge is facing you and fold the paper in half, left to right.

2Step 2

Then fold the left and right sides over to meet this middle crease. Unfold.

3Step 3

Fold the top two corners down to meet the outer creases, making two triangles.

4Step 4

Fold the top edges in to the center crease. Unfold.

5Step 5

Fold the left and right sides in to meet the crease made in the previous step.

6Step 6

Refold the crease made in step 4.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Sky OmniWing fly?Sky OmniWing 能飞多远？

With a calm day and a level throw, expect only a short distance, since this design is not built for range. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

在无风天气、水平投掷的情况下，大约能飞 距离很短，这款机型本身并不以远距为目标。风力、纸张克重和折痕误差都会改变实际结果。

How long does the Sky OmniWing stay in the air?Sky OmniWing 能滞空多久？

A well-trimmed Sky OmniWing should hold a very brief flight; the design is not aimed at hang time. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

调整得当的 Sky OmniWing 可以保持滞空时间很短，这类机型并不追求滞空。从更高的位置轻投、保持两翼水平，都能延长滞空时间。

What type of paper airplane is the Sky OmniWing?Sky OmniWing 属于什么类型的纸飞机？

It is a distance and time aloft glider rated Easy difficulty. Fold it from Letter & A4 in 6 steps and keep a pair of scissors handy.

它是一款远距滞空机型，难度为简单，用Letter & A4折 6 步，需要准备剪刀。

How should I throw the Sky OmniWing?Sky OmniWing 应该怎么投掷？

Launch gently at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high spot, and let it settle into a glide instead of throwing it hard.

以略向上扬的角度轻轻放出，最好从高处投掷，让它自然进入滑翔，不要用力硬抛。
