Expert Origami Glider Paper Airplane

Designed by an origami expert, this paper plane flies fast, stays up a long time and can do tricks. It is a master fold for long floating glides, shown in 10 steps (needs a small cut).

Metres, hang-time seconds, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table below — before the folding steps — with the fixed test protocol.

How to throw
Launch softly at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high point, and let the plane glide instead of forcing it.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Take one corner and crease it on a diagonal.

2Step 2

Trim the leftover bottom strip and open the sheet — you should have a square.

3Step 3

Turn the sheet so a corner points up, then fold the outer corners onto the midline.

4Step 4

Fold the tip down until it touches the horizontal line.

5Step 5

Bring both top corners in so they meet the midline.

6Step 6

Undo the previous fold, then accordion-tuck the sides into a diamond.

7Step 7

Fold the bottom half of the diamond up as shown in the guide.

8Step 8

Fold the sides of the top piece in to the center as shown in the guide.

9Step 9

Crease the sheet plane in half towards you.

10Step 10

Finally, fold out both wings to complete the plane as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Expert Origami Glider fly?

The Expert Origami Glider is not scored for distance in this hangar — it is not built for range. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Expert Origami Glider stay in the air?

Our measured hang time is 5–7 seconds, #12 of 36 for time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Expert Origami Glider best for?

It is best for long floating glides. Folding skill is master. Use Letter & A4, 10 steps, needs a cut.

How should I throw the Expert Origami Glider?

Launch softly at a slight upward angle, ideally from a high point, and let the plane glide instead of forcing it.
