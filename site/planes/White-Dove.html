White Dove

If you really try, you can get this to slightly glide like a dove, but this creation is mostly decorative.

中等 Letter & A4 6 步 装饰不用剪刀

叠法步骤

1步骤 1

Fold one corner diagonally.

2步骤 2

Cut off the extra piece at the bottom, making a square piece of paper folded once to make a triangle.

3步骤 3

Fold the corner of the triangle over so about 1/3 of it is overlapping the paper behind and 2/3 is sticking out.

4步骤 4

Take one layer and fold it in the opposite direction as shown.

5步骤 5

Fold the paper plane in half.

6步骤 6

Fold one wing up at a slight angle.
