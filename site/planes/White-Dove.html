White Dove Paper Airplane

If you really try, you can get this to slightly glide like a dove, but this creation is mostly decorative. It is a intermediate fold for display and looks, shown in 6 steps (folds with hands only).

The White Dove is built more for shape and handling than for raw range or hang time.

How to throw
This design is made for display; if you fly it, use a gentle toss and expect a short, floaty descent.

Estimates assume a calm day, a level launch, and a well-trimmed plane.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Take one corner and crease it on a diagonal.

2Step 2

Trim the bottom offcut so you have a square folded once into a triangle.

3Step 3

Fold the triangle corner over so about one-third overlaps the sheet behind and two-thirds sticks out.

4Step 4

Take one layer and fold it the opposite way as shown.

5Step 5

Close the paper plane along the spine.

6Step 6

Raise one wing on a gentle angle.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the White Dove fly?

With a calm day and a level throw, expect only a short distance, since this design is not built for range. Wind, paper weight, and small crease errors all change the result.

How long does the White Dove stay in the air?

A well-trimmed White Dove should hold a very brief flight; the design is not aimed at hang time. Launching from a higher spot and keeping the wings level will extend it.

What is the White Dove best for?

It is best for looking great on a shelf. Folding skill is intermediate. Use Letter & A4, 6 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the White Dove?

This design is made for display; if you fly it, use a gentle toss and expect a short, floaty descent.
