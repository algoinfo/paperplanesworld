Vertical Loop Flyer Paper Airplane

This is a decent glider that we have turned into an acrobatic vertical loop flying paper plane. It is a beginner fold for tricks and loops, shown in 10 steps (needs a small cut).

The Vertical Loop Flyer is built more for shape and handling than for raw range or hang time. See the measured results table for how we score designs that do fly for distance or hang time.

Test conditions (same for every design we score): 70–80 gsm Letter or A4 printer paper; standing release at about 1.6 m shoulder height; calm indoor air (no wind); each thrower gets 10 attempts and we keep their best throw. Ranks compare only designs that cleared the scoring threshold under this protocol.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Lay the sheet landscape, crease left to right, then open it.

2Step 2

Fold the top-left corner into the centre so the crease lines up with the corner.

3Step 3

Repeat that fold on the right side.

4Step 4

Next fold the top edge down about half an inch (1.25 cm).

5Step 5

Fold the top edge down three more times.

6Step 6

Now close the plane toward yourself.

7Step 7

Fold out one wing so the crease is parallel to the belly of the plane, which should be about 1 inch (2.5cm) tall as shown in the guide.

8Step 8

Repeat with the other wing as shown in the guide.

9Step 9

Next, fold out both wing flaps as shown in the guide.

10Step 10

Finally, cut two slits on the ends of each wing as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Vertical Loop Flyer fly?

The Vertical Loop Flyer is not scored for distance in this hangar — it is not built for range. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Vertical Loop Flyer stay in the air?

The Vertical Loop Flyer is not scored for hang time here — the design is not aimed at time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Vertical Loop Flyer best for?

It is best for tricks and loops. Folding skill is beginner. Use Letter & A4, 10 steps, needs a cut.

How should I throw the Vertical Loop Flyer?

Use a quick wrist snap and a fairly firm throw so the plane has enough speed to complete its maneuver.
