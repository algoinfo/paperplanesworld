Pointed Classic Dart Paper Airplane

This classically styled, easy-to-fold paper airplane can fly over 100ft! It is a beginner fold for far, straight throws, shown in 12 steps (folds with hands only).

The Pointed Classic Dart is built more for shape and handling than for raw range or hang time. See the measured results table for how we score designs that do fly for distance or hang time.

How to throw
Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.

Metres, hang time, and hangar ranks are in the measured results table before the folding steps.

Folding steps

1Step 1

Crease the sheet lengthwise, then open it flat.

2Step 2

Turn the sheet over and fold the top edge down about 1 inch (2.5 cm).

3Step 3

Flip the sheet back and bring both top corners to the middle crease.

4Step 4

Fold the top triangle down so its diagonals line up with the sheet's top corners.

5Step 5

Fold that triangle back up so the tip meets the top edge.

6Step 6

Flip to the back and fold both top corners to the middle crease.

7Step 7

Unfold the previous step and then fold the top two corners to the new crease that you just created as shown in the guide.

8Step 8

Refold the crease that you made in step 6 as shown in the guide.

9Step 9

Gently separate the two layers at the tip of the airplane to reveal a small pocket. Tuck the front piece into the pocket as shown in the guide.

10Step 10

Fully insert the point into the pocket and press it flat as shown in the guide.

11Step 11

Close the model in half along its length.

12Step 12

Fold down each wing so the edge of the wing lines up with the bottom of the airplane as shown in the guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Pointed Classic Dart fly?

The Pointed Classic Dart is not scored for distance in this hangar — it is not built for range. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

How long does the Pointed Classic Dart stay in the air?

The Pointed Classic Dart is not scored for hang time here — the design is not aimed at time aloft. See the measured results table above the folding steps for metres, hangar rank, and the fixed protocol (70–80 gsm paper, ~1.6 m shoulder-height release, calm air, best of 10 throws per person).

What is the Pointed Classic Dart best for?

It is best for far, straight throws. Folding skill is beginner. Use Letter & A4, 12 steps, hands only.

How should I throw the Pointed Classic Dart?

Hold the plane level and throw with a smooth, firm push, aiming straight ahead rather than upward.
