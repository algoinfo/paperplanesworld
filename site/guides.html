Questions people ask most

Short answers first — open a full guide when you need the steps.

Why does my paper airplane dive or stall?纸飞机为什么俯冲或失速？

A dive from release usually needs both trailing edges bent up about 1 mm after you rule out a downward throw. A stall (climb, slow, drop) usually means too much upward bend or too steep a launch — flatten the bends and throw nearer level. Full pattern table: fix a flight.

一出手就俯冲：先排除朝下投，再两侧后缘上折约 1 毫米。抬头减速再掉的失速：多半是上折过大或投得太陡——减小上折、更水平投。完整对照表见修正飞行。

What is the easiest paper airplane for beginners?新手最好折哪款纸飞机？

Basic Dart is the easiest starter in this hangar: Easy difficulty, no scissors, and a rewarding distance flight when the centre crease is sharp. See dart or glider.

Basic Dart 是站内最易上手的入门款：简单难度、不用剪刀，中缝压实后也够远。详见飞镖还是滑翔。

What paper should I use for paper airplanes?折纸飞机用什么纸？

Use 70–80 gsm A4 or US Letter printer paper. Avoid card stock. Compare papers only with the same design and alternating throws — choosing paper.

用 70–80g 的 A4 或 Letter 打印纸，别用卡纸。比纸时必须同一机型并交替投掷——见选纸。
