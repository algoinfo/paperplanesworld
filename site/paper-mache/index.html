Paper mache projects worth starting this week

If you searched for paper mache projects, begin with shapes that need only newspaper, paste, and overnight drying: a balloon mask, a small pinata, or a bottle-core volcano. Skip thick bowl molds until your paste consistency is reliable.

  • One-evening mask (2–3 strip layers)
  • Classroom volcano (see the full volcano guide)
  • Mini pinata for a party favor

Mask

Blow a balloon to head size, cover with three overlapping strip layers, leave eye/nose cutouts until dry, then pop the balloon. Paint after the shell feels light and hard. Use the same paste ratios as the recipe page.

Pinata

Build 4–5 layers on a balloon, embed a hanging loop under the last wet layer, dry fully, cut a fill hatch, then seal. Decorate only after the shell no longer feels cool or soft.

FAQ

What are good paper mache projects for beginners?

Start with a bowl-free classroom classic: a small mask on a balloon, a pinata shell, or a science-fair volcano. Use the flour-and-water paste recipe first, then pick a project that can dry overnight before painting.
