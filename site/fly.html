Each 3D model is drawn to the proportions of its design. Drag to rotate and check the wings and nose from the side, top, and front.

The test flight simulates a glide using that plane's estimated range and hang time, so you can try different launch angles and find the best throw.

Once it lands, open the folding guide, fold your own, and compare it with the simulation to see whether your creases and symmetry match.
